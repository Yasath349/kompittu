Traditional Sri Lankan Flavours Combined With Modern Luxury

Welcome to Kompittu Restaurant & Bakers, a local food destination in Anuradhapura serving authentic Sri Lankan cuisine and delicious bakery creations. We focus on fresh ingredients, generous portions, affordable prices, and friendly service to create a memorable dining experience.

Our journey began with a simple mission: to preserve and celebrate Sri Lankan local culinary history. Our signature dishes, including our famous Kottu and Fried Rice options, are prepared using traditional local techniques mixed with high-quality ingredients. Furthermore, our bakery collection features classic bakes like Kimbula Buns and Malu Paan, freshly baked daily in our custom stone ovens.

Our Core Philosophy & Commitment

At Kompittu Restaurant & Bakers, we believe that food is more than just nourishment — it is a bridge to our culture, history, and community. We take great pride in sourcing fresh, local spices and seasonal vegetables from Anuradhapura's local farmers, supporting the local ecosystem while keeping our carbon footprint low.

100% Fresh Spices

We do not use artificial preservatives or tasting powders. Our spice blends are hand-ground daily to preserve the authentic Sri Lankan taste.

Warm Hospitality

Every customer is welcomed as a guest in our home. Friendly, polite service is our promise to make you feel comfortable and valued.

Daily Fresh Baking

Our ovens start firing at 4:30 AM every morning. We ensure our bakery collection items are served fresh, warm, and soft.
